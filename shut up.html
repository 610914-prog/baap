More ways to stay prepared

Your safety, one place.

Tools for preparedness, communication and awareness. Some capabilities depend on your device, network and connected services.

📡

1. Offline SOS fallback

Rakshaa can keep a local record while offline and open your phone's SMS app with an alert for a saved guardian. Internet-based calls and live tracking may not work offline.

7. Smartwatch integration

Smartwatch SOS requires a supported companion app or device integration. This web app cannot claim to pair with or trigger a watch without that integration.

8. Self-Defence Academy

Learn prevention-first skills. Prioritise leaving unsafe situations, getting to a public place and contacting trusted help.

  • Notice exits and move towards well-lit, populated places.
  • Use a clear voice and set boundaries when safe to do so.
  • Keep distance, avoid confrontation and seek help early.
  • Share your route and check-in plan with a trusted person.
  • Learn first aid from a qualified instructor.
